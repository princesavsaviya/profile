Featured Product

TethrLink

GPL v3 · Open Source · M1–M7 Shipped

Active GitHub Live Site

Your tablet. Your second screen.
Zero Wi-Fi.

TethrLink streams a virtual display from your Linux PC to an Android tablet over direct USB tethering. A GStreamer pipeline encodes frames (JPEG or H.264) and pushes them over a private 192.168.42.x USB subnet. A GTK4 + Libadwaita desktop app with three-tab dashboard and system tray manages everything.
