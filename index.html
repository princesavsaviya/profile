TethrLink

GPL v3 · Open Source · v1.0 Shipped

Active GitHub Live Site

Your tablet. Your second screen.
Zero Wi-Fi.

TethrLink streams a virtual display from your Linux PC to an Android tablet over direct USB tethering. A GStreamer pipeline encodes frames via JPEG or H.264 over a private 192.168.42.x USB subnet. A full GTK4 + Libadwaita desktop app with 3-tab settings panel and system tray manages everything.
